Building Dashboards That Solve
Business Problems

I got into Power BI the way most analysts do by getting frustrated with static spreadsheets that couldn't answer the next question someone asked in a meeting. Since then, every dashboard I build starts the same way: what decision is this meant to support, and for whom?

My M.Sc. in Data Science gave me the statistical and modelling foundation. I'm now looking for a full-time role as a Power BI Developer, BI Developer, Data Analyst, or Data Scientist somewhere I can keep building the bridge between raw data and the decision it should inform.
