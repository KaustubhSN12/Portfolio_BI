Three Executive Dashboards: Call Centre, Retention & Workforce

A set of three connected Power BI dashboards built to give PwC's leadership a single, consistent view across call centre performance, customer retention risk, and workforce diversity — metrics that had previously lived in three separate reports.

Overview

Completed as part of PwC Switzerland's Power BI Job Simulation, this project involved building three interactive dashboards covering call centre operations, customer retention, and workforce diversity each designed to be read by an executive in under a minute.

Business Challenge

Leadership needed a consistent way to monitor call centre performance, spot customers at risk of churning, and track workforce representation without leadership having to interpret three differently-formatted reports built by three different teams.

Dataset

Call centre operational logs, customer account and subscription records, and internal workforce demographic data, each cleaned and shaped in Power Query and connected through a shared data model.

Dashboard Screenshots

Business Questions Answered

  • Is call centre performance improving or declining month over month?
  • Which customer segments carry the highest retention risk?
  • Where are the gaps in workforce representation, and at which levels?

Insights

  • The Call Centre Trends dashboard consolidated queue times, resolution rates, and agent performance into a single monthly view instead of three separate operational reports.
  • The Customer Retention dashboard surfaced the customer segments carrying the highest churn risk, so retention efforts could be targeted rather than blanket.
  • The Diversity & Inclusion dashboard gave leadership a consistent, repeatable way to track representation across levels of the organization.

Recommendations

  • Review the Customer Retention dashboard monthly alongside account management, not just at quarter-end.
  • Set representation targets directly against the D&I dashboard so progress is measurable, not anecdotal.
  • Standardize future reporting on the shared data model built here, rather than rebuilding it per team.

Technology Used

Power BIPower QueryDAXExcel

Lessons Learned

Building three dashboards off one shared model, rather than three independent files, was the real exercise here it meant getting the data model right the first time, since every downstream measure depended on it. It's the same discipline a real consulting engagement demands: get the foundation right, and the reporting layer becomes fast to build and easy to trust.
